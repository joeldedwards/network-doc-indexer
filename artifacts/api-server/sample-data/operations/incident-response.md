# Service Incident Response

When a service incident is reported, record the affected service, the first observed time, and the impact on customers.

## First response

- Notify the on-call lead.
- Use the incident channel for status updates.
- Keep a timestamped record of decisions and workarounds.
- Share a customer update only after the incident lead reviews it.

After recovery, schedule a blameless review and track follow-up actions.
